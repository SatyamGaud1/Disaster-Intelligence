Multispectral Drought Indices

Optical Earth Observation Methodology
1. NDVI Anomaly (35%) Measures relative chlorophyll loss and cellular collapse in crops: $\Delta\text{NDVI} = \text{NDVI}_{\text{current}} - \text{NDVI}_{\text{baseline}}$. Anomaly $\le -0.30$ signals severe agricultural drought.
2. NDWI Canopy Moisture Deficit (30%) Compares Near-Infrared (Band 8) with Shortwave-Infrared (Band 11). Negative values signify depleted leaf water thickness and soil dryness.
3. Vegetation Condition Index (20%) Evaluates current crop state against historical multi-year records: $\text{VCI} < 20\%$ marks acute historical drought percentiles.
4. Regional Agrozone Clustering (15%) DBSCAN with a 25 km radius clusters contiguous agricultural land parcels into prioritized regional intervention zones.
