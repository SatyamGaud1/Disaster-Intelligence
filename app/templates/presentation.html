Disaster Intelligence Platform

Satellite-Based Multi-Hazard Monitoring, Incident Prioritization & Emergency Response Platform

Version 2.0

🔥 Forest Fire Monitor

Active thermal anomaly detection via NASA FIRMS (VIIRS/MODIS), spatial DBSCAN clustering, and Random Forest risk estimation.

🌊 Flood Inundation Monitor

Copernicus Sentinel-1 C-band SAR backscatter drop detection coupled with NASA GPM IMERG 24h precipitation feeds.

🌾 Drought & Crop Stress

Copernicus Sentinel-2 MSI multispectral NDVI tracking, soil temperature anomalies, and precipitation deficit modeling.

🌀 Tropical Cyclone Tracking

NOAA NHC & IMD RSMC track surveillance, historical vs forecast cone of uncertainty, and Saffir-Simpson & IMD classifications.

🚑 Emergency Response & Helping Organization Tracking

Automated nearest organization lookup using Haversine spherical math across 8 agency categories (NDRF, SDRF, Fire, Hospitals, Ambulance, NGOs, Relief, Rescue). Complete 9-stage lifecycle tracking from Pending to Completed with audit trails and dedicated responder portal.

System Architecture Highlights

  • Backend: Python 3.11, Flask application factory, SQLite with WAL mode & foreign key constraints.
  • Frontend: Leaflet.js interactive maps, vanilla JavaScript, dark navy command-center styling.
  • Machine Learning: Scikit-learn Random Forest multi-factor risk models with cross-validation.
  • Zero Paid APIs: 100% free, public, and open scientific data standards.
